# WellnessGuard - Competitor Analysis

Comprehensive analysis of the screen wellness market landscape.

---

## Market Overview

- Employee monitoring software market: **$12.3B by 2033**
- Computer vision syndrome affects **75-90%** of computer users
- Average worker spends **7+ hours/day** in front of screens
- ROI: Every $1 in wellness = **$3.27** in reduced medical costs

---

## Direct Competitors (AI Blink/Eye Tracking)

### 1. BLiiNK AI
**Website:** [bliink.ai](https://www.bliink.ai/)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Features | Blink rate tracking, posture monitoring, screen distance check |
| Technology | On-device AI (privacy-first) |
| Platforms | macOS (M1/M2), Windows |
| Pricing | $3.99-8.33/month (Personal), Custom (Business/Enterprise) |
| Languages | English, French, German |

**Reported Results:**
- 41% decrease in neck/back pain
- 75% better posture
- 53% improvement in eye dryness
- 20% productivity boost (after 3 months)

**Weaknesses:**
- Mac-only for M-series chips initially
- Limited advanced features
- Takes time to show results

**Key Insight:** This is our closest competitor. They've validated the market.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Market Validated:** BLiiNK at $4-8/month proves willingness to pay
2. **Privacy is Table Stakes:** All serious competitors emphasize on-device processing
3. **Timer Apps are Free:** Can't compete on basic breaks - must add intelligence
4. **Flow State Awareness is Differentiator:** LookAway and DeskRest marketing this
5. **Enterprise is Underserved:** No focused screen wellness for B2B
6. **Cross-Platform Matters:** Mac-only limits market significantly
7. **Glasses Problem Not Solved:** No competitor mentions handling glasses well
