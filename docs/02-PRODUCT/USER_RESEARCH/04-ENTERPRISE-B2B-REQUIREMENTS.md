## What Enterprises Buy

### Tier 1: Core Requirements

#### 1. Health Risk Assessments
**What:** Online questionnaires + biometric data analysis

**Components:**
- Diet and nutrition assessment
- Exercise habits evaluation
- Stress level measurement
- Sleep quality tracking
- Ergonomic risk factors
- Mental health screening

**Output:**
- Individual risk scores
- Personalized recommendations
- Aggregate company-level insights

---

#### 2. Real-Time Analytics Dashboard
**What:** HR/manager view of program effectiveness

**Must-Have Metrics:**
- Participation rates (by department, location, role)
- Health outcome tracking
- Program engagement trends
- ROI/cost-impact reporting
- Absenteeism correlation
- Turnover risk indicators

**Critical Features:**
- Real-time data (not weekly reports)
- Role-specific views (HR vs Manager vs Executive)
- Exportable reports
- Benchmark comparisons

---

#### 3. Gamification & Challenges
**What:** Team-based wellness competitions

**Popular Challenge Types:**
- Step challenges
- Break compliance challenges
- Posture improvement challenges
- Department vs department competitions
- Company-wide events (marathons, wellness weeks)

**Required Elements:**
- Challenge leaderboards
- Team formation tools
- Community chat/forums
- Reward/incentive management
- Badge/achievement systems

---

#### 4. Mental Health Support
**Priority:** Governments now mandate workplace mental health resources (US DOL initiative March 2024)

**Components:**
- Stress management tools
- Burnout risk indicators
- Mental health resources library
- Optional anonymous support channels
- Manager visibility into team stress levels (aggregated only)

## Privacy & Compliance Requirements

### Data Privacy
- **GDPR compliance** (EU employees)
- **HIPAA compliance** (US health data)
- **SOC 2 certification** (security standards)
- Clear data retention policies
- Employee consent management
- Right to deletion

### Critical Privacy Principles for Eye Tracking
1. **No raw images stored or transmitted**
2. **Only aggregated metrics shared with employer**
3. **Individual data never visible to HR/managers**
4. **Employee controls what data is shared**
5. **Clear opt-in/opt-out at any time**

## Pricing Models Enterprises Expect

### Per-Employee-Per-Month (PEPM)
| Tier | Price Range | Features |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Basic | $1-3 PEPM | Break reminders, basic analytics |
| Standard | $3-6 PEPM | + Challenges, integrations |
| Premium | $6-12 PEPM | + Predictive analytics, custom content |
| Enterprise | Custom | + Dedicated support, custom integrations |

### Annual Contracts
- Typically 1-3 year commitments
- Volume discounts at 500+, 1000+, 5000+ employees
- Implementation fees separate ($5K-50K)

## Buyer Personas

### Primary: HR Director / VP of People
**Cares About:**
- Employee engagement metrics
- Turnover reduction
- Healthcare cost management
- Compliance/legal requirements
- Easy implementation

**Objections:**
- "Will employees actually use it?"
- "How do we measure ROI?"
- "What about privacy concerns?"

---

### Secondary: Benefits Manager
**Cares About:**
- Integration with existing benefits
- Vendor management simplicity
- Cost predictability
- Employee satisfaction scores

---

### Influencer: IT/Security Team
**Cares About:**
- SSO/SAML support
- Data security standards
- Infrastructure requirements
- Privacy/compliance

**Potential Blockers:**
- Camera access on work devices
- Data residency requirements
- Security certifications
