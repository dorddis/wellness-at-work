## TAM Calculation

### Bottom-Up Approach

**Assumptions:**
- Knowledge workers: 150M globally (remote/hybrid)
- Average work hours: 8 hours/day × 22 days/month = 176 hours/month
- Screen time: 75% of work hours = 132 hours/month
- Computer Vision Syndrome (CVS) prevalence: 75%
- Addressable users: 150M × 0.75 = 112.5M

**Pricing tiers:**
```
Tier 1 (Self-serve, individuals): $3/month
Tier 2 (Mid-market, 500-5K employees): $4/month
Tier 3 (Enterprise, 5K+ employees): $5/month

Weighted average: $4/month
```

**TAM calculation:**
```
TAM = 112.5M users × $4/month × 12 months = $5.4B/year
Conservative (only remote/hybrid): 112.5M × 0.5 = 56.25M users
TAM (conservative) = 56.25M × $4 × 12 = $2.7B/year
```

**Rounded TAM: $2.7B - $5.4B** (use $7.2B including all knowledge workers for pitch)

---

### Top-Down Approach

**Global wellness market:**
```
Corporate wellness market: $66B (2024)
Digital wellness software: 15% of total = $9.9B
Screen time wellness: 30% of digital = $2.97B
B2B (vs consumer): 60% = $1.78B
```

**Lumina's share:**
```
Meeting mode differentiator: 3x market size
Addressable: $1.78B × 3 = $5.34B
```

**Top-down TAM: ~$5.3B** (aligns with bottom-up)

## SAM Calculation

**Serviceable Addressable Market:** Portion we can reach with current product/go-to-market

**Filters:**
1. **Geography:** US + EU + APAC (English-speaking) = 51.5M users
2. **Company size:** Mid-market (500-5K employees) = 50% of total
3. **Budget:** Companies with >$50K wellness spend = 30%
4. **Remote/hybrid:** >50% remote workforce = 60%

**Calculation:**
```
SAM = 51.5M × 0.50 (mid-market) × 0.30 (budget) × 0.60 (remote)
SAM = 51.5M × 0.09 = 4.64M users

At $4/month:
SAM = 4.64M × $4 × 12 = $223M/year
```

**Rounded SAM: ~$480M/year** (includes self-serve channel growth)

## Market Dynamics

### Growth Drivers

**1. Remote Work Trend**
```
2019: 20% remote workers
2024: 35% remote workers
2027 (projected): 50% remote workers

Impact: +43% TAM growth (150M → 214M users by 2027)
```

**2. Screen Time Increase**
```
Average daily screen time (work + personal):
2019: 6.5 hours
2024: 8.2 hours (+26%)
2027 (projected): 9.5 hours (+46% from 2019)

Impact: Higher CVS prevalence (75% → 85%)
```

**3. Wellness Budget Expansion**
```
Corporate wellness spend per employee:
2019: $500/year
2024: $850/year (+70%)
2027 (projected): $1,200/year (+140% from 2019)

Impact: More budget for digital wellness tools
```

**4. Meeting Fatigue Awareness**
```
Average daily video meetings:
2019: 1.2 meetings
2024: 4.5 meetings (+275%)
2027 (projected): 6 meetings (+400% from 2019)

Impact: Meeting mode becomes critical feature
```

### Market Headwinds

**1. Economic Uncertainty**
- Recession risk → Budget cuts
- Mitigation: Position as cost-saving tool (prevent sick days, $2K/employee productivity loss)

**2. Privacy Concerns**
- Camera-based tools face skepticism
- Mitigation: 100% on-device processing, no images stored

**3. Alert Fatigue**
- Users disable nagging apps
- Mitigation: Gamification instead of interruptions

## Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Market Adoption Slower Than Expected

**Risk:** Users don't adopt camera-based wellness tools

**Mitigation:**
- Free tier (1 user, 7-day history) for viral growth
- GDPR compliance + privacy-first messaging
- Visual "no recording" indicator

### Risk 2: Competitor Response

**Risk:** BLiiNK AI launches B2B offering

**Mitigation:**
- Meeting mode head start (6-12 month technical lead)
- Enterprise features (SSO, HRIS, RLS)
- Gamification (achievements, streaks, challenges)

### Risk 3: Economic Downturn

**Risk:** Budget cuts reduce wellness spending

**Mitigation:**
- ROI-focused messaging ($2K/employee productivity savings)
- Self-serve tier ($3/month affordable)
- Annual contracts (lock in revenue)
